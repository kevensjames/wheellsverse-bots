Sol — Non-Custodial Terms & Risk Disclosure

Version 2026-07-03

Template pending legal review. This disclosure describes how Sol works and the risks of participating. It is provided for clarity and is not a substitute for the final, attorney-reviewed terms that will govern your use of Sol. Do not rely on this as legal advice.

1. Sol is software, not a bank

Sol is a coordination tool for rotating savings circles (also called ROSCAs, tandas, susus, or hui). Sol never accepts, holds, pools, custodies, routes, or transfers your money. Sol is not a bank, money transmitter, payment processor, escrow agent, lender, or investment service. Your money never passes through Sol or any account Sol controls.

2. Members pay each other directly

All money moves directly between members, outside of Sol, using your own payment methods (for example Zelle, CashApp, Venmo, or cash). Sol only records what members report: when you "mark paid," you are stating that you sent money directly to another member; when a recipient "confirms," they are acknowledging they received it. Sol is never in the middle of that transfer and cannot move, reverse, refund, freeze, or guarantee any payment.

3. What Sol does

  • Creates and organizes circles, invitations, and contribution schedules.
  • Calculates the payout rotation and reminds members of what is due.
  • Records member-reported payments and two-sided confirmations.
  • Shows a reputation signal derived from members' own payment history.

4. Risks you accept

  • A member may not pay. A rotating savings circle depends entirely on members paying each other. Sol does not guarantee any payment and will not reimburse you if a member fails to pay. You could receive less than you contributed, or nothing.
  • No insurance. Money coordinated through Sol is not held anywhere and is not insured by the FDIC, NCUA, or any other party.
  • Records rely on member honesty. "Marked paid" and "confirmed" reflect what members report; Sol cannot independently verify that money actually moved.
  • Disputes are between members. If a payment is disputed, Sol records the dispute but does not adjudicate, mediate, or resolve it. Resolution is up to the members.
  • Only join circles with people you trust. Treat participation like an informal agreement among people you know.

5. Not financial, legal, or tax advice

Sol does not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Participating in a savings circle may have tax or legal consequences that vary by jurisdiction. You are responsible for understanding and complying with the laws that apply to you.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate payment handles and report payments truthfully and promptly.
  • Do not use Sol for unlawful purposes, fraud, or to coordinate anything you are not permitted to under the laws that apply to you.
  • Keep your account secure.

7. Acceptance

By accepting these terms you confirm that you understand Sol is non-custodial, that members pay each other directly, and that you accept the risks above — including that you may not be repaid. You may need to re-accept if these terms are materially updated.
