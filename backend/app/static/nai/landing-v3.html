why_this_exists

Tuesday, 11pm, 40 minutes into a Stripe webhook bug. Asked ChatGPT for a second opinion. Got: "You've reached your GPT-4 limit. Try again in 3 hours."

I was paying $20/mo for the privilege of being told to wait. That night I started building KAI.
