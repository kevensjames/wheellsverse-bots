# NEXORA — Founding-Creator Waitlist (Kit / ck.page)

Paste-ready copy for the hosted Kit landing page at `wheellsverse.ck.page/nexora`
(the page itself must be created in the Kit dashboard — Kit's API can't create
hosted landing pages). The tag + welcome sequence are wired by
`scripts/nexora_waitlist_kit.py`.

Product truths this copy is grounded in (the platform we built): subscriptions,
pay-per-view posts, tips, live tipping, DMs; **creators keep 90% (10% platform)**;
$20 minimum payout; transparent balance.

---

## Landing page

**Badge:** Now onboarding founding creators

**Headline:** Keep 90% of what you earn.

**Subhead:** NEXORA is the creator platform where your subscribers, pay-per-view
drops, tips, live rooms, and DMs all live in one place — and the money is yours.
No 20–50% cuts. No payout games.

### Why creators are switching
- **90/10, not 50/50** — you keep 90% on subscriptions, PPV, and tips.
- **Every revenue stream, one dashboard** — recurring subs, locked posts, tips, live tipping.
- **You own your audience** — export anytime; your fans, your data.
- **Payouts you can track** — transparent balance, $20 minimum, no surprise holds.

### How it works
1. **Claim your handle** — join the founding-creator waitlist.
2. **Set up in minutes** — profile, subscription price, first locked post.
3. **Get paid** — fans subscribe, unlock, and tip; you withdraw.

**Founding-creator offer:** First 100 creators lock in reduced platform fees for
life + priority verification.

**CTA button:** Claim my founding spot →
**Form fields:** Email *(required)* · First name · "I'm a…" (Creator / Fan)
**Microcopy:** No spam. We'll email you the moment your invite is ready.
