# Cold DM template — partnership outreach

**Use case:** Cold-pitch newsletter operators, podcast hosts, micro-influencers in the AI / trading / passive-income / crypto space. Goal is a 50% lifetime rev-share on referred AI Stock Alerts subscribers.

**Reply rate target:** 5–10% (anything below 3% means the list, not the message).

---

## The 4-line template (use as-is, then personalize line 1)

> Hey [first name] — been reading [Newsletter Name] for [N] months, the [specific recent piece] piece on [specific topic] was the one that made me subscribe.
>
> I run AI Stock Alerts ($19/mo, ~[real number] members, daily AI-curated trade signals). Looking for ~5 partner publications to refer subs to in exchange for **50% lifetime rev share** — that's $9.50 every month for every active subscriber you send, paid via Stripe Connect.
>
> No exclusivity, no minimum traffic. I'd send you a tracking link, a 200-word blurb you can drop in your next issue, and pre-made graphics. First publication who replies gets the lowest tracking-link slot (#1).
>
> Worth a 10-min call this week, or just want me to ship the assets and let you decide?

---

## Personalization rules (do not skip)

1. **Line 1 must reference a SPECIFIC piece they wrote in the last 60 days.** Not "love your stuff." Open the latest 3 issues, find one paragraph that genuinely struck you, mention the exact topic.
2. **Line 2 number must be real.** If you have 47 subs, say 47. If 0, say "early-stage, looking for the first 5 partners — terms get worse for partners 6+."
3. **Tracking link slot #1 is real social proof** — only say it for the first 5 you send to, then change to "first 10".

---

## Sending mechanics

- **Channel order**: 1) X DM (highest open rate), 2) the email listed in their newsletter footer, 3) LinkedIn last (worst).
- **Cadence**: 10/day max. More than that and you'll get flagged on every platform.
- **Follow-up**: ONE follow-up after 4 days. "Bumping in case this slid past — happy to ship the assets and let you skim, no call needed." Then drop them.
- **Track everything**: Add `?utm_source=dm-{handle}&utm_medium=newsletter&utm_campaign=partner-pitch-{date}` to every link in the message itself. Even if they don't reply, if they check the link, that tells you the message landed.

## Reply playbook (the easy part)

- **"Yes, send assets"** → ship: (a) 200-word blurb, (b) 800x418 banner image, (c) tracking link with their handle. Done within 24h.
- **"What's the audience size?"** → answer truthfully + share the WheellsVerse Discord or Telegram member count + LinkedIn proof if you have it.
- **"50% feels low/high"** → "Standard for digital subscriptions in this niche — happy to do 60% for the first 30 days as a kickstart so you have a data point."
- **No reply** → drop after one bump. Don't take it personally; volume is the strategy.

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't send a Loom/video first. Those get ignored. Text-first.
- ❌ Don't lead with the rev-share number — lead with WHY them, then the offer.
- ❌ Don't link to your homepage. Link to a SPECIFIC trade-thread tweet that proves you do this seriously.
- ❌ Don't follow more than once. Two bumps = you look desperate.

## Tracking — this matters more than the pitch

In a Notion/Airtable/sheet, log every send with: `date | handle | platform | personalization line | link clicked Y/N | replied | reply summary`. After 100 sends you'll know which line-1 hooks land and which die. Iterate.
