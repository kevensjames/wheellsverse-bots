Pricing FAQ

Why is it so cheap compared to agencies?
Most agencies charge $3,000-15,000 for a small business site because they overhead a designer, project manager, and account exec on every project. We use a proven template adapted to your business and the same person (Jay) handles your account end-to-end. No middlemen, no hours billed for kickoff calls.
Is the $497 really one-time? No hidden fees?
Yes. $497 charges once at signup. The only ongoing cost is the $49/mo if you keep maintenance — and that's cancel-anytime. No setup fees, no "platform fees", no per-page charges.
Do I have to subscribe to maintenance?
No. You can pay $497, get the site files, and host it yourself for free. About 90% of customers stay on maintenance because it's hassle-free — but it's your call.
What if I cancel?
If you cancel within 7 days and we haven't delivered the live site yet, full refund. After delivery, the $497 is non-refundable but the $49/mo is cancel-anytime. You keep the files.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit cards via Stripe. We don't accept bank transfers, checks, or crypto for the initial $497 — too much fraud exposure.
