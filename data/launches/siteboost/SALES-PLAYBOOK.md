# SiteBoost Sales Playbook

What to say at every step of the funnel from cold-email reply through close.
The scripts below are written for a single solo operator — no team, no CRM
training required. Read once, internalize, then improvise.

---

## Step 1 — When a reply lands in your inbox

### A. Auto-responder (fires within 60 seconds)

This goes out automatically via Instantly's reply detection or via a Gmail
filter rule. Hook is: ANY reply to a SiteBoost cold email → fire this.

**Subject**: `re: your site preview — got time for a 15-min call?`

```
Hi {first_name},

Glad you replied. Two quick things:

1. The preview at https://preview.hello.wheellsverse.com/{slug} is yours either way —
   no obligation, no expiring link.

2. If you want to talk through making it live, grab any 15-min slot here:
   https://calendly.com/<your-handle>/15min

I'll have your site preview pulled up on the call so we can tweak anything
live. Most calls end with the site going live within 48 hours.

If you want to skip the call and just ask a question by email, hit reply.
I read everything.

— Jay
SiteBoost AI
```

**Why this works**:
- Confirms the preview is theirs no matter what (lowers anxiety)
- Gives Calendly + "just reply" — two paths, low friction
- 48-hour timeline anchors urgency without pressure
- 15-min ask is small (1-hour calls scare local business owners)

### B. Reply-tag rules (manual or automated via Gmail labels)

Tag each incoming reply within 30 minutes:

| Reply text contains | Tag | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| "interested", "tell me more", "what's the cost" | `interested` | Wait for them to book Calendly OR follow up in 24h |
| "how much", "pricing", "$" | `pricing-question` | Reply with **Pricing Reply (Section C)** below |
| "send to my partner/wife/manager", "I'll show this to" | `forwarded` | Reply: "Great — happy to talk to them too. Same Calendly link." |
| "not interested", "remove me", "STOP" | `unsubscribe` | Unsubscribe + delete contact. No further reply. |
| "who are you", "is this real" | `trust` | Reply with **Trust Reply (Section D)** below |
| "we already have a site" | `mistake` | Reply: "Apologies — pulling the file. Best of luck." Apologize + remove. |

## Step 2 — The 15-minute discovery call

Goal: close on the call, get $497 charged before hangup. Average converting
call lasts 12 minutes.

### Pre-call (do this 5 min before)

- Open their preview at `preview.hello.wheellsverse.com/<slug>` in your browser
- Open their Google Maps listing in another tab
- Have Stripe payment-link tab ready: `https://buy.stripe.com/<siteboost-497>`
- Have notes app open for their content (services, hours, photos to collect)

### Call structure (15 minutes)

**Minute 0-1 — Warm open (DO NOT pitch yet)**

> "Hi {first_name}, thanks for grabbing time. I pulled up the preview I sent
> you on my screen — did you have a chance to look at it on your phone?"

Let them answer. If yes: ask what they liked. If no: open it together on the
call, walk them through it.

**Minute 1-3 — Listen, find the pain**

Ask ONE of these (read the room):

- "What made you reply? Is the website something you've been meaning to do for a while?"
- "Are you getting most of your customers from word of mouth right now, or do people find you online too?"
- "Have you ever had someone call expecting you to have a website?"

GOAL: Get them to say the pain in their own words. "Yeah I've been meaning
to do this for 2 years" is worth $497.

**Minute 3-6 — Walk through the preview, make ONE change live**

> "Let me show you something. Watch this — I'm going to change the headline
> right now."

Edit the preview live (the file is a simple HTML, you can hot-edit). They
see it change in their browser. This single demo is the close.

If they say "wow that's it?", you're closing.

**Minute 6-9 — Land the offer**

> "Here's how this works: $497 today gets the site live on your own domain
> within 48 hours. After that, $49 a month covers hosting and any small
> changes — new hours, photos, holiday updates. Cancel anytime, no contract.
>
> If your domain isn't registered yet, I'll handle that too — just costs
> $12 a year, I'll add it to the invoice."

PAUSE. Don't fill silence. Let them respond.

**Minute 9-12 — Handle objections (Section 3 below)**

**Minute 12-13 — Send the Stripe link**

> "Okay perfect. I'm going to drop the payment link in chat / send it to
> your email right now — should hit in about 10 seconds."

Send the Stripe link via Calendly chat (if Zoom) or email it during call.

**Minute 13-15 — Set the next 48-hour expectation**

> "Once that processes, you'll get an email with a short form — takes 5
> minutes. Just need your business hours, top 3 services, and 2-3 photos
> if you have them (phone snapshots are fine). After you send that, I'll
> have the site live within 48 hours. Sound good?"

Hang up.

## Step 3 — Objections + responses

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "$497 is a lot" | "Totally fair. Compare it to one slow week of customers — most clients I work with say one missed phone call costs them more than that. And there's no contract — you'd just be out the one-time fee, the $49/mo is month-to-month." |
| "Can I think about it?" | "Of course. While you're thinking, do you want me to keep the preview live, or take it down? I usually take previews down after 30 days." (This is the *gentle* anchor — most agree to "keep it live" which is a soft commitment.) |
| "Send me a written proposal" | "Sure — I'll email you the full scope right after we hang up. Most of what we discussed is already in the preview itself. Should I include the payment link so you can decide on your own time?" |
| "Can I pay in installments?" | "Yes — $250 today, $250 next month, then $49/mo from month 3. Same delivery timeline." (Only offer if they push twice — full price is preferred.) |
| "My nephew said he could build me one" | "He probably can. The difference is mine is live in 48 hours and I handle hosting + updates so you never think about it. If timing isn't a factor, he'll probably do great. If you want it live this week, that's what I do." |
| "Will I own it?" | "Yes — full ownership. You can take the site files and move them anywhere if you ever cancel. The $49/mo just covers me hosting and maintaining it for you." |
| "I don't want to lock into a subscription" | "No lock-in. You can pay $497 just for the site, get the files, and host it yourself for free. The $49/mo is purely if you want me to handle hosting + tweaks. About 9 out of 10 customers keep the subscription because it's hassle-free." |
| "I'm not techy" | "That's exactly why this works — you don't have to be. Email me changes when you want them ('add new menu item', 'change hours'), I make them, done." |
| "What if I don't like it?" | "7-day money-back if I haven't delivered the live site in 48 hours. After delivery, no refund — but I revise unlimited times in the first 30 days at no extra cost until you're happy." |
| "Send me references" | "Happy to. Three customers in your area: [link to hello.wheellsverse.com/work]. You can email any of them — I won't be CC'd." (Build this page early — even 3 references is enough.) |

## Step 4 — Post-call follow-up

### If they paid on the call

Within 5 minutes of hangup, send:

```
Subject: ✓ Got your $497 — here's what happens next

Hi {first_name},

Confirmed! Your $497 just hit my account. Here's the next 48 hours:

1. Within the hour: you'll get a short intake form. 5 min to fill out.
   It collects: hours, services, photos.

2. Within 48 hours: your site is live on your domain. I'll send the URL +
   a 2-minute video walking through how to request changes.

3. In 30 days: your first $49 monthly charge clears. You can cancel
   anytime from your account.

Intake form: https://hello.wheellsverse.com/intake?customer={customer_id}

Any questions before then, just hit reply.

— Jay
```

### If they DIDN'T pay on the call but are interested

Wait 4 hours, then send:

```
Subject: re: SiteBoost — payment link if you decided

Hi {first_name},

Nice talking earlier. Whenever you're ready, here's the payment link:
https://buy.stripe.com/<siteboost-497>

Same offer we discussed: $497 + $49/mo. Live in 48 hours.

If anything came up that needs another conversation, just reply.

— Jay
```

If no reply after 3 days, ONE final follow-up:

```
Subject: re: site preview — closing the file

Hi {first_name},

Last note from me — going to close the file on this one unless I hear back.
Preview stays live at https://preview.hello.wheellsverse.com/{slug} for another 30
days if anything changes.

All the best,
— Jay
```

Then stop. Do not bother them again.

## Step 5 — Volume targets per week (for a solo operator)

| Activity | Time | Target |
|---|---|---|
| New cold sends (after warmup done) | 30 min/day × 5 | 250 emails/week |
| Reply triage + auto-responder confirmations | 15 min/day | 15-25 replies/week |
| 15-min discovery calls | 12-15 min × 4-8 | 4-8 calls/week |
| Site delivery (post-payment, 30-45 min per site) | 45 min × 2-4 | 2-4 sites/week |
| Total active hours | | ~6 hours/week |

At 6 hours/week and the funnel rates from PRODUCT-BRIEF.md:
- 250 emails × 6% reply = 15 replies
- 15 × 40% book call = 6 calls
- 6 × 50% close = **3 sales/week × $497 = ~$1,500/week one-time + $147/week new MRR**

That's break-even by week 2, profitable by week 3. Scale by adding more cities.

## Closing principles (read every Monday morning)

1. **Speed kills competition.** Reply within 2 hours of any cold-email response. Most agencies take 24-48 hours — you take 2.

2. **The preview IS the proof.** Never pitch features in the call. Pitch the THING they're already looking at. "Look at this. You like it? $497."

3. **Don't sell maintenance up front.** Land the $497, then 30 days in they're already invested. The $49/mo charge is automatic, not a separate sale.

4. **Lose the small fights.** $50 off, free domain, custom logo. Give those away. The lifetime customer is worth $1,183 — don't lose them over $50.

5. **Refund fast.** If someone wants out in week 1, full refund, no questions. Bad-fit customers in your pipeline cost you more than $497.

6. **The first 10 customers are practice.** Don't optimize until you have 10 done. Until then you're learning what objections actually arise, what content they actually send in intake, what tweaks they actually request.
