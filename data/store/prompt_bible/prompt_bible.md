# The Prompt Bible — v1

**200 copy-paste ChatGPT prompts across 20 categories.**
Built by J.K. Blaze for WheellsVerse members.

## How to use
Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any LLM. Replace anything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]` with your specifics before hitting send.

## 01. Business Strategy

1. Act as a strategy consultant with 20 years of experience. Review my business idea — [DESCRIBE IDEA] — and tell me the three most likely reasons it will fail in year one. For each reason, give one concrete countermeasure I can implement this week.
2. My business made $[REVENUE] last month with [NUMBER] customers. Identify the single highest-leverage change I could make in the next 30 days to 2× my revenue. Show your reasoning before the recommendation.
3. I'm deciding between [OPTION A] and [OPTION B] for my business. List five questions I should ask myself before deciding, ordered from most to least important, with why each question matters.
4. Analyze my top three competitors — [LIST COMPETITORS] — and identify one underserved niche each of them is missing. Rank the niches by how fast I could capture them with minimal capital.
5. I have [BUDGET] to spend on growing my [BUSINESS TYPE] this quarter. Draft a 90-day allocation plan with specific line items, expected ROI per item, and a kill-switch rule for each.
6. Write a one-page business model canvas for a [TYPE OF BUSINESS] targeting [AUDIENCE], optimized for under $5k in startup cost. Make each section specific enough that I could execute from it tomorrow.
7. My business has plateaued at $[REVENUE]/mo for three months. Interview me with the five questions a turnaround CEO would ask to diagnose the bottleneck — ask one at a time and wait for my answer.
8. Design three pricing tiers for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] using the decoy-effect principle. Explain which tier is designed to make the others look like better deals, and why.
9. List 10 "unsexy" businesses that are profitable, boring, and scalable — businesses that real owners quietly make $500k+/year from. For each, state the main barrier to entry and the main skill required.
10. Pretend you're my CFO. Given my revenue of $[X], costs of $[Y], and goal of $[Z], tell me the three numbers I should track weekly and the threshold at which each becomes a red flag.

## 02. Marketing & Sales

1. Write a Facebook ad (headline, primary text, CTA) for [PRODUCT], targeted at [AUDIENCE], using the PAS framework (Problem–Agitate–Solution). Max 100 words, conversational tone.
2. I sell [PRODUCT] for $[PRICE]. Write five objection-handlers for the most common reasons people don't buy — price, trust, timing, self-doubt, relevance. One sentence each.
3. Design a 14-day launch sequence for [PRODUCT]. Include email subject lines, content themes per day, and the CTA escalation pattern. Format as a table.
4. Write three headline variants for my landing page selling [PRODUCT]. Variant 1 = curiosity hook, Variant 2 = direct promise, Variant 3 = contrarian angle. Then pick the winner and explain why.
5. Give me 10 hooks to open a TikTok/Reels video about [TOPIC]. Each hook must work in under 3 seconds and include pattern interrupts.
6. I need to move [NUMBER] of [PRODUCT] in 30 days. Design a scarcity-and-urgency campaign that feels honest, not pushy. Include the exact language for each scarcity trigger.
7. Write a cold-outreach DM opener for [PLATFORM] that has a <5% chance of being ignored. Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Max 2 sentences. No fake compliments.
8. What are the top three emotional triggers that make [AUDIENCE] buy [PRODUCT TYPE]? For each, give one headline that uses the trigger well and one that abuses it.
9. Reverse-engineer the marketing of [SUCCESSFUL COMPETITOR]. Identify their core promise, pricing strategy, traffic source, and the one thing I could ethically borrow.
10. I'm running a $[BUDGET] ad campaign to promote [PRODUCT]. Should I front-load spend in week 1 or spread evenly across 4 weeks? Give pros/cons of each and your recommendation for a new product vs. a proven product.

7. Give me 5 carousel post structures that drive saves on Instagram. For each, show slide-by-slide content for my topic: [TOPIC].

## 08. Sales Calls & Outreach

2. Turn this rambling pitch into a 30-second elevator pitch: "[PASTE PITCH]". Lead with the one-line result I deliver.

## 14. Stock & Investing

2. Explain the PEG ratio with a specific example using [STOCK]. When is it useful vs. misleading?
3. I want to start dollar-cost averaging into [STOCK/ETF] at $[X]/month. What are the three behavioral rules I need to agree to before starting?
4. Walk me through how to read a 10-K for a company like [COMPANY]. Which five sections matter most, and what red flags should I look for in each?
5. Compare Roth IRA vs. traditional 401k for someone making $[INCOME]. Give me the break-even calculation and the emotional/practical factors most articles skip.
6. What's a "dividend trap" and how do I avoid it? Show me one real historical example where the dividend yield looked great but the stock still crashed.
7. Explain options trading in one paragraph to a beginner. Then give me the three strategies I should master before touching anything else, and one I should never touch.
8. Here's my portfolio: [LIST HOLDINGS]. Diagnose concentration risk, sector exposure, and tell me the one rebalancing move with the best risk/reward.

10. I have $[X] sitting in a brokerage account uninvested. Given the current market conditions, should I invest it all at once or spread over 6 months? Show both math and psychology.

## 15. Coding & Dev

4. I'm debugging [ISSUE]. Here's what I've tried: [LIST]. Based on the symptoms, list three hypotheses ordered by likelihood, with a quick test for each.
5. Convert this bash script into idiomatic Python: [PASTE]. Preserve behavior, prefer standard library, add error handling where failure would be silent.
6. Design my git branching strategy for a team of [N] devs shipping weekly. Include naming conventions, PR rules, and what goes to main vs. stay in feature branches.
7. Review this API design for REST correctness and developer ergonomics: [PASTE ENDPOINTS]. Flag anything that will cause pain at scale.
8. I need to write tests for this function: [PASTE]. Generate 5 tests covering: happy path, boundary, bad input, upstream failure, concurrent call.
9. Explain [NEW TECHNOLOGY] to someone who's been writing [STACK] for 10 years. Focus on what's genuinely new vs. rebranded.
10. Teach me the mental model for [CONCEPT: eg. "async/await"] by first showing why synchronous code fails, then how this concept fixes it. End with one thing I will always get wrong at first.

3. Design a weekly KPI dashboard for [BUSINESS TYPE]. Pick 5 metrics, define each, explain what "normal" looks like, and set thresholds for alert vs. crisis.
4. I'm about to A/B test [CHANGE]. Help me calculate required sample size, set a cutoff date, and write a pre-registration of what I'll conclude at each possible outcome.
5. Review my SQL query for correctness, performance, and readability: [PASTE]. Suggest indexes if they'd help, no rewrite if not needed.
6. Turn this messy Excel export into a clean analytical structure. Here's a sample row: [PASTE]. Show the pivoting logic and the three cleanup steps in order.
7. I'm seeing a [METRIC] drop of [X]% this week. Build me a diagnostic tree: start with the three most common root causes and the specific slices to check for each.
8. Explain the difference between correlation and causation using a real example from [MY INDUSTRY]. Show why the obvious conclusion is often wrong.
9. Build a simple cohort analysis for [METRIC] by [DIMENSION]. Show me the query shape and what to look for in the resulting table.
10. I have [N] surveys from my customers. Write the prompt I should feed back to you to extract the top 5 themes, plus the sentiment and urgency of each.
