The Secret Sauce Behind REITs and Dividend Stocks

Both REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts) and dividend stocks offer an accessible entry into the world of passive income. The key mechanism? They pay you to hold them. Dividend stocks distribute a portion of a company's earnings to shareholders, while REITs pay out at least 90% of their taxable income as dividends, often resulting in higher-than-average yields. Both options allow you to grow your wealth passively, but the approach and outcomes can differ significantly.
