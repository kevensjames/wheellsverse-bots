Why REITs and Dividend Stocks Work

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and dividend stocks are popular choices for passive income investors because they offer regular payouts, typically on a quarterly basis. Here's why they work:

  • REITs: These are companies that own, operate, or finance income-generating real estate. By law, they must distribute at least 90% of their taxable income as dividends to shareholders. This makes REITs a reliable source of income, often with higher yields than traditional stocks.
  • Dividend Stocks: These are shares in companies that return a portion of their earnings to shareholders. High-quality dividend stocks, often from well-established companies, can provide a steady income stream and potential for capital appreciation.
Both options can potentially help you reach that $500/month goal, but they come with different risks and benefits.

  • Dividend Stocks: Look for companies with a strong history of paying and increasing dividends, such as Johnson & Johnson (JNJ), Procter & Gamble (PG), or Coca-Cola (KO).
