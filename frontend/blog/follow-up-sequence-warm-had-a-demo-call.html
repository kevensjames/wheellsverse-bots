OBJECTION HANDLING

  • "We need more time" → Acknowledge their timeline, offer to check back in a few weeks, and share additional resources in the meantime.
  • "Budget is the issue" → Highlight potential ROI and flexible payment plans that align with their budgetary needs.
  • "We're going with a competitor" → Respect their decision, but ask if there are any areas they felt were lacking that WheellsVerse could address differently.
  • "Not a priority right now" → Acknowledge shifting priorities, and suggest a future follow-up to revisit when the timing might be better.
