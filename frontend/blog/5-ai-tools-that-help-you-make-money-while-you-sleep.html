5 AI Tools That Help You Make Money While You Sleep

In the fast-paced world of entrepreneurship, finding ways to generate passive income is crucial. With the advancements in AI tools in 2025, automating workflows has never been easier. Here are five AI workflow automation tools that can help you earn money while you sleep.

Why This Matters Now

2025 has seen a significant shift in AI capabilities, making it easier for busy entrepreneurs and creators to automate various aspects of their businesses. These tools not only save time but also create income streams that work for you around the clock.

Tool Roundup

1. Zapier

  • What it does: Connects over 5,000 apps to automate repetitive tasks without coding.
  • Best use case: Automatically add new email subscribers to your CRM and send them a welcome email series.
  • Pricing: Free tier available; paid plans start at $19.99/month.
  • Standout feature vs alternatives: Multi-step Zaps allow for complex workflows that other automation tools may not support.
  • Who should use it: Ideal for small to medium-sized business owners looking to streamline workflows. Not recommended for those with minimal tech skills, as setup can be complex.

2. Integromat (Make)

  • What it does: Advanced automation platform that connects apps and automates workflows visually.
  • Best use case: Automatically generate reports from your e-commerce sales data and send them to your team.
  • Pricing: Free plan available; paid plans start at $9/month.
  • Standout feature vs alternatives: Visual flow builder allows for intricate workflows that are easy to adjust.
  • Who should use it: Great for tech-savvy entrepreneurs and developers. Not suitable for those who prefer simplicity over complexity.

3. Adalo

  • What it does: No-code app development platform that helps you create mobile apps with automation features.
  • Best use case: Build a subscription-based mobile app that generates recurring revenue.
  • Pricing: Free tier with limited features; paid plans start at $50/month.
  • Standout feature vs alternatives: User-friendly interface that allows non-developers to create fully functional apps.
  • Who should use it: Entrepreneurs looking to monetize app ideas without coding experience. Not for those who want highly customized apps.

4. Trello with Butler Automation

  • What it does: Project management tool that automates tasks and workflows within boards.
  • Best use case: Automate task assignments and reminders for team projects, keeping everything organized and efficient.
  • Pricing: Free basic plan; paid plans start at $5/user/month.
  • Standout feature vs alternatives: Built-in automation via Butler allows for custom commands that enhance productivity.
  • Who should use it: Teams and project managers who need to streamline tasks. Not ideal for individuals who work solo.

5. ChatGPT API

  • What it does: AI-powered chatbot that can handle customer inquiries, generate content, and more.
  • Best use case: Providing 24/7 customer service on e-commerce websites, handling FAQs and inquiries without human intervention.
  • Pricing: Pricing varies based on usage; starting at $0.002 per token.
  • Standout feature vs alternatives: Natural language processing capabilities that allow for more human-like interactions.
  • Who should use it: E-commerce businesses and content creators looking to enhance customer engagement. Not suitable for those with a low volume of customer inquiries.

How to Use These Tools for Income

Content Creation Monetization

Leverage tools like ChatGPT API to create engaging content for your blog or videos. Use Zapier to share new posts automatically across social media, driving traffic and potential ad revenue.

Service Business Automation

Automate your service business with Integromat and Trello. For example, if you run a marketing agency, automate client onboarding processes and task assignments to improve efficiency, allowing you to take on more clients with less effort.

Affiliate Income Opportunities

Utilize Zapier to track affiliate sign-ups automatically. You can also integrate financial tools like Robinhood for automated investing, allowing you to earn passive income through stocks. For crypto enthusiasts, Coinbase can automate your trading strategies based on market conditions.
