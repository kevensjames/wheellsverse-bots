TICKET CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

PRIORITY LEVELS

P0 — CRITICAL (Respond in: 1 hour)

  • Criteria: Issues that severely impact the user's ability to use the WheellsVerse AI platform, affecting multiple users or critical business functions.
  • Examples:
- Platform down or major functionality inaccessible - Severe billing errors leading to immediate financial impact - Data loss or corruption
  • SLA: First response 1 hour | Resolution 4 hours
P1 — HIGH (Respond in: 4 hours)
  • Criteria: Issues that significantly impede user experience or cause major disruptions to business operations but are not critical.
  • Examples:
- Technical bugs affecting core features - Urgent account issues preventing login - Refund requests needing immediate processing
  • SLA: First response 4 hours | Resolution 24 hours
P2 — MEDIUM (Respond in: same-day response)
  • Criteria: Non-urgent issues that require attention but do not hinder operations.
  • Examples:
- Feature requests that are not time-sensitive - How-to questions or requests for guidance - Minor account issues
  • SLA: First response same day | Resolution 3 business days
P3 — LOW (Respond in: 3 business days)
  • Criteria: General inquiries or feedback that have little to no immediate impact.
  • Examples:
- General suggestions or feedback - Non-urgent inquiries about platform capabilities - Inquiries related to past transactions or general information
  • SLA: First response 3 business days | Resolution 5 business days
