Support Ticket Classification and Response System for WheellsVerse AI Platform

TICKET CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

PRIORITY LEVELS

P0 — CRITICAL (Respond in: 1 hour)

  • Criteria: Issues that significantly impact the customer's ability to use the platform or involve sensitive data breaches.
  • Examples:
- Billing issues that prevent access to the platform. - Major technical bugs that cause system outages. - Security vulnerabilities.
  • SLA: First response 1 hour | Resolution 4 hours
P1 — HIGH (Respond in: 4 hours)
  • Criteria: Issues that affect a significant portion of users or critical functionalities but do not halt all operations.
  • Examples:
- Technical bugs affecting features used by multiple users. - Urgent account issues (e.g., locked account). - Refund requests for significant amounts.
  • SLA: First response 4 hours | Resolution 8 hours
P2 — MEDIUM (Respond in: same-day response)
  • Criteria: Issues that affect individual users but do not severely impact their overall experience.
  • Examples:
- Feature requests for enhancements that are non-urgent. - How-to questions concerning existing functionalities. - Minor account issues (e.g., password reset).
  • SLA: First response same day | Resolution 24 hours
P3 — LOW (Respond in: 3 business days)
  • Criteria: Non-urgent inquiries or suggestions that do not require immediate action.
  • Examples:
- General inquiries about the platform. - Feedback on user experience. - Non-critical feature requests.
  • SLA: First response 3 business days | Resolution 5 business days
