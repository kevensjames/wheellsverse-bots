Support Ticket Classification and Response System for WheellsVerse AI Platform

TICKET CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

PRIORITY LEVELS

P0 — CRITICAL (Respond in: 1 hour)

  • Criteria: Immediate system outage, severe impact on all users, data breaches, or critical billing issues that could lead to revenue loss.
  • Examples:
- System completely down for all users - Unauthorized access detected - Major billing errors affecting multiple accounts
  • SLA: First response 1 hour | Resolution 4 hours
P1 — HIGH (Respond in: 4 hours)
  • Criteria: Significant impact on user experience or functionality, but not a complete outage. Issues that require immediate attention to prevent escalation.
  • Examples:
- Significant bugs affecting major features - Account access issues for multiple users - Billing discrepancies for individual accounts
  • SLA: First response 4 hours | Resolution 1 business day
P2 — MEDIUM (Respond in: same-day response)
  • Criteria: Moderate impact on user experience, general questions, or requests that don’t lead to immediate revenue loss or system issues.
  • Examples:
- Feature requests or suggestions - How-to questions regarding platform usage - Minor account issues
  • SLA: First response same-day | Resolution 3 business days
P3 — LOW (Respond in: 3 business days)
  • Criteria: Low-impact issues that do not affect current operations or are general inquiries.
  • Examples:
- General inquiries about the platform - Non-urgent feature requests - Feedback on minor usability issues
  • SLA: First response 3 business days | Resolution 1 week
