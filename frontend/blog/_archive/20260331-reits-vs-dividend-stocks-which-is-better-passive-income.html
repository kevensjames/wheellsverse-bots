Why REITs and Dividend Stocks Work for Passive Income

Both REITs and dividend stocks allow you to earn income without selling your assets. They essentially pay you for holding them, offering a steady cash flow. Here’s why they’re effective:

  • REITs: REITs are companies that own, operate, or finance real estate that produces income. By law, they must distribute at least 90% of their taxable income to shareholders as dividends, making them a reliable income source.
  • Dividend Stocks: These are shares in companies that pay out a portion of their earnings to shareholders. Companies with solid, consistent earnings often offer dividends, providing a regular income stream.
Both options benefit from the power of compounding, where reinvesting your dividends can lead to exponential growth over time.
