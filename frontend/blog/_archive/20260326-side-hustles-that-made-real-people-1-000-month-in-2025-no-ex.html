Step-by-Step Launch Plan

Step 1: What to Do in the First Hour

  • Identify Your Niche: Spend an hour researching trending topics or areas of interest. Use tools like Google Trends or AnswerThePublic to assess demand.
  • Choose Your Platform: Decide where you'll sell or promote your product. Etsy, Gumroad, and Teachable are excellent for beginners selling digital products like e-books, courses, or printables.

Step 2: First Week Milestones

  • Create a Minimal Viable Product (MVP): Draft an outline or prototype of your digital product. It doesn't have to be perfect—just something to test the waters.
  • Set Up Your Storefront: Use platforms like Etsy or Gumroad to set up your digital shop. Both are user-friendly and require no upfront fees until a sale is made.
  • Register for Affiliate Platforms: If incorporating investment insights, sign up with Webull or Coinbase to integrate affiliate offers.

Step 3: First Month Targets

  • Launch Your Product: Publish your digital product and promote it via social media or blogging. Leverage free platforms like Instagram or TikTok to reach potential customers.
  • Gather Feedback: Encourage buyers to leave reviews. Use this feedback to tweak and improve your product.
  • Expand Your Reach: Consider creating a simple website or blog using a no-code tool like WordPress.com or Webflow to attract organic traffic.

The #1 Mistake People Make

The biggest mistake is not validating your product idea before investing significant time and resources. Always test your concept with a minimal viable product (MVP) and gather feedback early on. This approach saves time and ensures you’re meeting market demand.
