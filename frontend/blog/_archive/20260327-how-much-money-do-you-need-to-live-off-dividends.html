Why Dividends Work: The Core Mechanism

Dividends are payments made by a corporation to its shareholders, usually as a distribution of profits. When you own shares of a dividend-paying company, you're entitled to a slice of those profits. This makes dividends an excellent passive income source. Companies often pay dividends quarterly, and with enough shares, these payments can cover your expenses, like a mini paycheck.

Why is this effective? Because you're letting money work for you rather than working for money. Dividend investing leverages the power of compound interest and time, creating a snowball effect that can grow your income steadily.
