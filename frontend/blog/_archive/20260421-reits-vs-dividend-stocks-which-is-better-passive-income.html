Why REITs and Dividend Stocks Work for Passive Income

Both REITs and dividend stocks provide income streams by distributing profits to investors.

  • REITs: These are companies that own, operate, or finance income-generating real estate. By law, they must distribute at least 90% of their taxable income to shareholders, making them attractive for consistent income.
  • Dividend Stocks: These are shares in companies that distribute a portion of their earnings to shareholders regularly, typically quarterly. Dividend stocks often come from established companies with stable earnings.
The core mechanism here is simple: you invest money upfront, and in return, you receive regular income without needing to sell your shares. This is the magic of compounding — your money works for you, generating more money over time.

2. Set Your Investment Strategy

REIT Strategy:

Dividend Stock Strategy:

  • Select Stocks: Target companies with a track record of increasing dividends, strong cash flow, and solid financial health.
  • Diversify: Choose stocks across different sectors to mitigate risk.
  • Reinvest Dividends: Use dividend reinvestment plans (DRIPs) to compound your earnings.
