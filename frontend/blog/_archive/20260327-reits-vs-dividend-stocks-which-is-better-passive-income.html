Why This Works: Understanding REITs and Dividend Stocks

Before choosing between REITs and dividend stocks, it’s important to understand why they are effective passive income generators:

  • REITs: These are companies that own, operate, or finance income-producing real estate. By law, they must distribute at least 90% of their taxable income to shareholders annually, making them attractive for income-seeking investors.
  • Dividend Stocks: These are shares in companies that regularly return a portion of their earnings to shareholders. Companies with a stable dividend history can provide consistent income.
Both options offer a way to earn without selling your investments, making them ideal for those seeking a steady income stream.
