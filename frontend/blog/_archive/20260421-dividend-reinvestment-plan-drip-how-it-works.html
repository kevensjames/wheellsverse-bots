Why DRIPs Work: The Mechanism Behind the Strategy

DRIPs allow you to automatically reinvest the dividends you earn from your investments back into more shares of the same stock or fund. This reinvestment helps compound your returns over time, as each dividend payment buys more shares, which in turn earn more dividends. It's like planting a tree that grows more trees — your income multiplies as your investment base expands.

Core Benefits of DRIPs:

  • Compounding Returns: Reinvested dividends purchase additional shares, leading to exponential growth.
  • Cost Averaging: Buying shares over time can lower your average purchase price and mitigate risk.
  • No Additional Fees: Many DRIPs are offered by companies or brokers without additional transaction fees.
