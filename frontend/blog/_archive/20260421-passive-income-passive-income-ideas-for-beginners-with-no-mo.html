1. Start a Blog or YouTube Channel

Step-by-Step Process

1. Choose a Niche: Focus on a topic you're passionate about—be it cooking, tech reviews, or personal finance. 2. Create Content: Consistently produce high-quality content. Aim for at least one post or video per week. 3. Monetize: Once you have an audience, join affiliate programs or use ad networks like Google AdSense.

Real Numbers and Timelines

  • Investment: $0 (apart from time)
  • Timeline: 6-12 months to see significant income
  • Potential Earnings: Can range from $100/month to $10,000/month depending on traffic and engagement.

Pros and Cons

Pros:

  • Creative freedom
  • Build a personal brand
Cons:
  • Time-intensive upfront
  • Requires consistent effort

Investing in dividend stocks is a classic method to generate passive income. With platforms like Robinhood, you can start investing without any commission fees.
