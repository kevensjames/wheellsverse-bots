Why This Strategy Works

The key to successful passive income is leveraging investments that pay you regularly. Both REITs and dividend stocks distribute earnings to investors, making them ideal for passive income seekers. Here's how they work:

  • REITs: These are companies that own or finance income-generating real estate. Legally required to distribute 90% of taxable income to shareholders, REITs can offer high yield returns.
  • Dividend Stocks: Companies that pay a portion of their earnings to shareholders as dividends. Typically, blue-chip stocks, known for their stability, are popular dividend payers.
Both options provide a regular income stream, but which is better for you depends on your goals, risk tolerance, and investment style.
