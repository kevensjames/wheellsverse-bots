Why This Works

Dividend investing is a strategy where you earn consistent income from your investments in dividend-paying stocks. Essentially, companies share a portion of their profits with you, the shareholder, on a regular basis — typically quarterly. This method of earning is reliable, as many companies have a strong history of dividend payouts and even increasing them over time.

Here's why it works:

  • Regular Income: Unlike capital gains, which depend on selling stocks at the right time, dividends provide regular income without having to sell.
  • Compounding Potential: Reinvesting dividends can significantly grow your portfolio over time.
  • Less Emotional Investing: Dividend investors often focus on income rather than stock price fluctuations, leading to a more stable investment approach.
