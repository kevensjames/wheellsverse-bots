Privacy Policy

WheellsVerse  |  Effective date: March 1, 2025  |  Last updated: March 26, 2025

WheellsVerse ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WheellsVerse platform, which automates AI-driven content creation and social media publishing for its users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — name, email address, and username when you create an account.
  • Social platform tokens — OAuth access tokens for platforms you connect (TikTok, Twitter/X, etc.). These are stored securely and used solely to publish content on your behalf.
  • Content data — text, captions, and media generated through our platform.
  • Usage data — bot run history, publish logs, and performance metrics.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and device info collected automatically when you access our dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and publish content to connected social platforms on your behalf.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the WheellsVerse platform.
  • To display your account status, publishing history, and analytics in the dashboard.
  • To communicate important service updates or security notices.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. TikTok Integration

When you connect your TikTok account, WheellsVerse uses the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok identity (user.info.basic scope).
  • Upload and publish AI-generated videos to your TikTok profile (video.upload and video.publish scopes).

We access only the scopes you explicitly authorize. We do not read your TikTok messages, followers, or private data beyond what is required to post content. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings or from the WheellsVerse dashboard.

4. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored in your environment configuration and are retained until you disconnect a platform or request deletion. Content outputs are stored locally and on our hosting infrastructure for up to 12 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted transport (HTTPS), environment-variable-based secret storage, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party services including OpenAI, TikTok, Twitter/X, and ConvertKit. Your use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

7. Children's Privacy

WheellsVerse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.
