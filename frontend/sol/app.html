Before you join

  • Joining a circle means recurring contributions each cycle until it completes.
  • Payout timing depends on the circle's rules, your payout position, and completing your contributions — it isn't guaranteed on a specific date.
  • A circle is a savings arrangement, not an investment — there's no profit, yield, or guaranteed return, and SOLCIRCLE is a software platform, not a bank.
  • Tracked savings-goal progress is separate from actual circle contributions.

Community guidelines & safety
  • Never share bank, card, account, or routing numbers, identity or verification details, passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials, or government ID numbers.
  • Community posts are not official account-status updates, and member opinions are not financial, credit, or investment advice.
  • For anything about your account, use Payments, Bank, Verify ID, Membership, or Notifications.
  • See something unsafe, misleading, or abusive? Use Report on a post or comment to send it for review — reporting isn't an instant judgment.
  • Posts can't be edited or deleted after posting yet, so please write with care.
