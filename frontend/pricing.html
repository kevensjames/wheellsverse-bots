Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No penalties, no questions asked.
What is NarAI exactly?
NarAI is an AI assistant built by Jhon Kevens D Wheeler — creator of WheellsVerse. It combines the power of multiple AI models (Claude, GPT-4o) into one interface with persistent memory, code mode, and web search. Think ChatGPT + Claude + Perplexity in one, smarter.
Does NarAI remember me?
Yes — on Pro, Max, and Ultra plans. NarAI learns your name, goals, projects, and preferences over time. Every conversation builds on the last. Free tier has 7-day memory only.
What's the difference between plans?
Free = 10 msgs/day, basic AI. Pro = 100 msgs/day + persistent memory. Max = 500 msgs/day + code mode + all models. Ultra = unlimited everything + API access.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Secure, encrypted, PCI-compliant. We never store your card details.
